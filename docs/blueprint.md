# **App Name**: RotaConecta

## Core Features:

- Landing Page: Landing page inspired by Fretebras with sections for header, sub-header with registration form, categories, explanations, app promotion, blog, and standard footer.
- Driver Registration: Driver registration with vehicle details (plate, RNTRC, type, body, capacity, tracker, locator) and document uploads (CNH, CRLV).
- Company Registration: Company registration form capturing name, commercial email, WhatsApp, CPF, date of birth, and password.
- Login Screen: Login screen with access division for drivers, transporters, and brokers using CPF and password, with 'stay connected,' 'forgot password,' and 'need help' options.
- Freight Expiration: Freights expire in 3 days
- Operational Cost Calculator: Operational cost calculation tool: calculates operational costs based on origin, destination, vehicle type, tolls, fuel, and cargo details, referencing ANTT tables for minimum values.
- VIP System: Premium Driver System: VIP drivers gain priority access (30 minutes) to view freights, along with 30 free operational cost calculations. (no datastore)

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated blue (#29ABE2) to reflect trust and logistics efficiency.
- Background color: Light blue (#E0F7FA), offering a clean and professional backdrop.
- Accent color: Vibrant green (#90EE90) for CTAs, highlighting priority actions, and indicating success.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for a modern, neutral, and readable interface.
- Use clear, simple icons for vehicle types, cargo, and operational steps.
- Prioritize a responsive layout for accessibility across devices, ensuring a seamless experience for both companies and drivers.
- Employ subtle transitions and loading animations to enhance user experience.
